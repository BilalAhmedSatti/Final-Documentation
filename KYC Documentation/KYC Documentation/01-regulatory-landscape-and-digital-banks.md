> Research cut-off: September 2026. Prefer SBP primary sources. Statuses change — re-verify before board or build decisions. Not legal advice.

## 2. Regulatory landscape

### 2.1 Licensing framework for digital banks

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Instrument | Licensing and Regulatory Framework for Digital Banks |
| Circular | BPRD Circular No. 01 of 2022 |
| Date | 3 January 2022 |
| URL | https://www.sbp.org.pk/bprd/2022/C1.htm |
| License types | **Digital Retail Bank (DRB)**; **Digital Full Bank (DFB)** |
| Mode | Conventional or Islamic (conventional may seek Islamic window with SBP approval) |
| Initial cohort | Up to **five** licenses |
| Channels | Primarily digital/electronic; limited physical presence only with SBP prior approval |

**Licensing stages (distinguish carefully):**

```text
Application → NOC → In-Principle Approval (IPA)
    → Operational readiness / restricted Pilot License
    → Commercial Digital Retail Bank License
    → (Optional later) progression toward DFB subject to framework conditions
```

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **NOC** | Regulatory no-objection to proceed in the licensing pipeline — **not** authority for commercial banking operations |
| **IPA** | In-principle approval to establish the digital bank and complete readiness |
| **Restricted / Pilot license** | Limited live operations under SBP conditions (customers, products, volumes) |
| **Commercial DRB license** | Authority for commercial digital retail banking (subject to conditions) |
| **Scheduled bank** | Separate SBP notification under SBP Act (e.g., clearing/settlement ecosystem participation) — related to but distinct from DRB license grant |

### 2.2 KYC / onboarding rule stack (current vs historical)

| Instrument | Date | Role today |
|------------|------|------------|
| AML/CFT/CPF Regulations for SBP-REs | Updated to 28 Nov 2022 (BPRD CL 33/2022) + later amendments | **Core CDD/EDD/PEP/TFS/STR/record-keeping** — still primary |
| Consolidated Customer Onboarding Framework | BPRD Circular No. 01 of 2025 (25 Jul 2025) | **Current consolidated onboarding** for banks, DFIs, MFBs, **Digital Banks**, EMIs |
| Customers’ Digital Onboarding Framework | BPRD Circular No. 02 of 2021 (revised 2022) | **Historical / superseded in large part** by Consolidated Framework — cite only with “superseded/partially superseded” label |
| Shared e-KYC Platform | BPRD Circular Letter No. 22 of 2023 (18 Dec 2023) | Industry shared KYC exchange (DLT, consent) |
| Mobile App Security Guidelines | PSP&OD Circular No. 01 of 2022 | Digital channel security (**A** for digital onboarding security section) |
| Digital banking product security | BPRD Circular No. 04 of 2023 | Security of digital banking products |
| Enterprise Technology Governance Framework | BPRD Circular No. 05 of 2017 | Tech governance / risk |
| AML Act, 2010 | Statute | Legal foundation for CDD, STR, FMU |

**Applicability note (Consolidated Framework §B):** Explicitly includes **Digital Banks**.

### 2.3 Risk-based AML architecture (AML regs)

SBP REs must apply a **risk-based approach** (Regulation–1): assess ML/TF/PF risk of customers, products, delivery channels, geographies; apply SDD/CDD/EDD accordingly; maintain CRP models; monitor and refresh.

Non-face-to-face / digital delivery is a recognized higher-risk delivery channel factor — mitigated by biometric/NADRA verification, screening, geo/IP, and EDD/video KYC where required (Consolidated Framework §§F–G).

### 4.1 Easypaisa Bank Limited

- **Path:** Microfinance bank → digital retail bank conversion under SBP digital bank framework.  
- **SBP:** Declared scheduled bank upon DRB license (notification 28 Jan 2025).  
- **Public KYC signals:** App signup with mobile + CNIC + CNIC issue date + OTP + T&Cs + PIN; biometric upgrade paths for higher limits (public FAQs/upgrade pages). See [04-digital-bank-kyc-benchmarks.md](04-digital-bank-kyc-benchmarks.md).

### 4.2 Mashreq Bank Pakistan Limited

- **Path:** Wholly owned subsidiary of Mashreq Bank PSC (UAE).  
- **SBP:** First **restricted license for pilot operations** as DRB (press 20 Feb 2025).  
- **Public KYC signals:** Mashreq Mobile App / NEO; CNIC/NICOP; mobile registered to customer; SNIC scan upload; digital FATCA/CRS; biometric/BVS & liveness language in T&Cs; EDD discretion. Islamic and conventional product variants exist publicly — our bank remains **Conventional DRB** for design purposes.

## Implications for our Conventional DRB

1. Expect the same **AML + Consolidated Onboarding** bar as other SBP REs, with digital-bank-specific fallbacks (video KYC + Verisys; third-party reliance with banks that have physical presence).  
2. Pilot phase may impose **customer/product/volume caps** — design KYC/account limits as configurable by regulatory phase.  
3. Benchmarking peers is useful for UX; **SBP text controls the control set**.  
4. Islamic-only peers (Raqami, possibly Buraq) do not change Conventional DRB KYC core, but may differ in product disclosures and Shariah governance (out of scope beyond note).
