# 22–24. Security, fraud, audit (EMI)

> EMI §19–21, §23; Consolidated §K; PS&EFT secrecy. Not legal advice.

## Security (**A**)

- Fidelity and secrecy of personal information.  
- CIA of data/services: international standards + SBP circulars.  
- **Systems audit** from SBP-approved panel **before pilot** and **annually**.  
- Security breaches: immediate notice to stakeholders including PSP&OD; detailed report **15 days** (Annexure F).  
- Major cyber: **48 hours** to PSP&OD.  
- BCP/DRP: drills **half-yearly** for first two years of pilot, **quarterly** thereafter; formal report.  
- Cloud: BPRD Circular 01/2023. No offshore outsourcing without SBP written approval.

## Fraud / impersonation

- Live photo + NADRA BV/Verisys.  
- 2FA on customer verification and ATM cash-out.  
- Device binding / SIM pairing **required** for enhanced (**A**); recommended earlier (**C**).  
- Real-time **all-transaction** alerts (**A**).  
- TMS.  
- Liveness/AI face match: **B/C**, not a BV substitute.

## Audit

Every KYC decision, limit upgrade, agent cash session, trust posting, and screening adjudication must be reconstructible. Independent director chairs Board Audit Committee (governance §10).

## Consumer protection (§20)

First-time / low-literacy customers: fraud, privacy, and **loss-of-service** protections; board-approved dispute process; **separate** agent-complaint track; awareness on instrument use and rights.
