# Appendix A — EMI regulatory requirement catalog

> Clause list with what each rule actually requires in product and ops. Narrative in [02-sbp-emi-kyc-cdd-framework.md](../02-sbp-emi-kyc-cdd-framework.md). **Not legal advice.** Confirm current text on sbp.org.pk before freezing a build.

**Class A** = mandatory SBP / law. Do not weaken in UX. This catalog is all **A**.

| ID | Source | Requirement | Class | What it means |
|----|--------|-------------|-------|----------------|
| A-E01 | PS&EFT Act / EMI §2–3 | E-money is a claim on the issuer, not a bank deposit | A | The customer holds **prepaid e-money**, a claim on this EMI, not a deposit under the Banking Companies Ordinance. Copy, receipts, and support must not say “account”, “savings”, or deposit insurance. Outstanding value sits in a **trust account**, not on the EMI’s own banking book. Wallet limits and CDD follow EMI Regulations, not digital-bank product tables. |
| A-E02 | EMI §7 | No banking/lending; no interest on e-money; no issue at a discount; no VCs | A | The EMI must not take deposits, lend the public’s money, or pay **interest / return / monetary value** just for holding e-money. Instruments are issued **at par**, never at a discount. Virtual currencies are out of scope. Product features that “grow your wallet balance” while idle, or cashback that is economically interest, need Legal sign-off before build. |
| A-E03 | EMI §8 | Four-stage licence: initiation / IPA / pilot / commercial | A | You cannot skip stages. **Initiation** → in-principle approval (**IPA**) → **pilot** (capped limits, including BV 200k) → **commercial**. Enhanced 1M and some limit exclusions are only after commercial licence plus a further PSP&OD application. The app must know which stage it is in; a pilot build must not show commercial 400k or 1M as if already approved. |
| A-E04 | EMI §11 | PKR 200m initial capital; ongoing OEB slabs; 10% security deposit at BSC | A | Licensing capital is **PKR 200 million**. Ongoing own-funds follow **outstanding e-money balance (OEB)** slabs. **10%** of relevant funds is a security deposit with SBP BSC. This is finance/treasury, not a KYC screen — but OEB reporting, trust sweeps, and wallet issuance must stay reconcilable so capital returns are true. |
| A-E05 | EMI §12.I–II | Minimum fields; 2 extra non-CNIC fields; live CNIC/photo where applicable; 2FA | A | At issuance collect at least: name; father/spouse; ID (CNIC, NICOP, POC, Passport, ARC, POR); mobile; residential address; **two fields not printed on the CNIC** (e.g. mother’s name, place of birth); and, where applicable, **live original CNIC and live digital photo**. CCOF makes live photo **A** for digital/remote open. Also collect Consolidated **Table-A** as applicable (purpose, SoI, FATCA/CRS, PEP, occupation). **§12.II: at least two-factor authentication** to verify the customer — OTP/2FA. A selfie is not 2FA and is not NADRA BV. |
| A-E06 | EMI §12.III | Verify + TFS pre-screen before relationship or payment use | A | Particulars in §12 must be verified, and the person (and associated parties) **pre-screened** against UNSC lists and ATA 1997 proscribed persons **before** you start the relationship **or** let them use payment services. A true match: no wallet, no load, freeze as required, written decline, STR/TFS ops — not a retry button. Screening after first spend is too late. |
| A-E07 | EMI §12.IV | Optional one credit; else close + STR | A | Policy **may** allow **one** inbound credit before credentials are fully verified. If verification then fails, **close** the instrument and file **STR**. This is not a standing anonymous prepaid. The system needs a one-credit flag, a timer, and a fail-closed path. Product copy must not look like “use first, KYC later.” |
| A-E08 | EMI §12.VII | One instrument per CNIC per EMI | A | An individual or entity may hold **only one e-money instrument with this EMI**. A second CNIC attempt is login/resume of the existing wallet, not a new issue. This is per EMI, not “one wallet in all of Pakistan.” CCOF uniqueness language aligns. Hash the ID, block parallel applications, and do not invent a second “lite” wallet for the same CNIC. |
| A-E09 | EMI §12.VIII | Real-time alerts all transactions | A | Every transaction gets a **real-time** customer alert — not only cash-out, not only above a threshold. Channel (push, SMS, email) is design **D**; existence of the alert is **A**. Alerts also support fraud dispute timelines. Do not batch “end of day” as a substitute. |
| A-E10 | EMI §14 | Pilot/commercial/enhanced/minor limit tables | A | **Payments and receipts are separate buckets.** Commercial: Verisys load **PKR 50,000**/month and cash-out **10,000**/day; NADRA BV load **400,000**/month (pilot BV **200,000**); BV cash-out from risk profile; ATM cash-out always **2FA**; agent cash-out BV or 2FA if BVS is a major challenge. Minors: guardian-linked, 50k Verisys or 400k BV freelancer, not the adult enhanced table. Limit **exclusions** (salary, inward remittance up to 1.5m, utility) need an SBP application and apply to BV holders, not minors. UI caps must read from this policy, not a bank debit-block table. |
| A-E11 | EMI §14.III | Enhanced: SoF, SIM pairing, TMS, CRP; not outsourced | A | After **commercial licence**, apply to PSP&OD for up to **PKR 1,000,000**. All four must hold: Annexure-J income/source of funds; **CNIC/SIM pairing of the device on which the instrument is opened**; TMS with robust scenarios (one-to-many, many-to-one, etc.); detailed **customer risk profiling**. Verification of these **must not be outsourced**. Device bind on ordinary 50k/400k wallets is **not** this clause — see pack security notes (C until Legal says BPRD 04 is A). |
| A-E12 | EMI §15 | Issue/redeem at par; cash-in BV; cash redeem BV | A | Issue the instrument **at par, without delay**, when funds are received. Fund via IBFT (any ADC) or **cash-in** at EMI/agent/ATM/bank **subject to biometric verification**. Redeem at par **any time, no charges**. **Cash redemption requires NADRA BV.** A Verisys 50k wallet can exist without BV; taking or giving cash still needs BV (agent cash-out may use 2FA only where BVS is a major challenge). |
| A-E13 | EMI §16 | Trust account; no co-mingle; trustee A-rated; 50% cap >300m OEB | A | Customer e-money must sit in a **trust account** at an **‘A’-rated** licensed bank (trustee). Do not co-mingle with the EMI’s operating money. When OEB exceeds **PKR 300 million**, additional diversification / 50% cap rules apply (see current §16 text). Product and ledger must be able to prove safeguarding; this is why “wallet balance” is not the EMI’s working capital. |
| A-E14 | EMI §17 | Agents SBP-approved; cannot issue wallets | A | Agents are **SBP-approved** distribution points for permitted services (typically cash-in/out), **not** issuers. The agent channel must **never** create a wallet (`POST /wallets` or equivalent). Cash-in: BV. Cash-out: BV or 2FA if BVS is a major challenge. Agent complaints have a **separate** track under consumer protection. Journey UJ-E12 documents the customer path; agent ops stay in file 10. |
| A-E15 | EMI §21–22 | Annual system audit; TMS; RBA; new-tech risk | A | **Systems audit** by an SBP-approved panel **before pilot** and **annually**. Automated **TMS** for ML/TF. Risk-based AML. **New product or technology** needs an ML/TF risk assessment **before launch**. Security breaches: notify including PSP&OD; detailed report in **15 days** (Annexure F); major cyber in **48 hours**. Do not treat TMS as “a vendor dashboard we might add later.” |
| A-E16 | EMI §24.II | Records ≥10 years; call recordings 1 year | A | Keep EMI records **at least 10 years** or as required by law. Call-centre recordings **1 year**. AML regs often cite **5 years** after end of relationship — implement the **stricter** applicable rule (Legal to confirm). Every KYC decision, limit upgrade, screening hit, and agent cash session must be reconstructible for audit. |
| A-E17 | BPRD C1/2025 | EMIs in Consolidated Framework; wallet limits still EMI regs | A | The Consolidated Customer Onboarding Framework applies to **EMIs** (scope §B). **Footnote 1:** e-money **limits and related wallet instructions stay in the EMI Regulations**. Use CCOF for how you onboard (identity hierarchy, live photo, TAT, tracking, resume). Use EMI **§14** for how much the wallet may load. Do not import bank debit-block or bank product caps as the EMI happy path. |
| A-E18 | BPRD C1/2025 | Live photo; BV ladder; video KYC if no presence; TAT 2 WD; tracking ID | A | Digital/remote open: **live photo** with valid ID. Identity: primarily **NADRA BV**; Verisys alternate for disability, unclear prints, age **>60**, NRP/POC abroad. EMIs **without physical presence**: recorded **video KYC + Verisys** (record why BV was not met) or third-party reliance on a bank with branches. Individual decision **TAT 2 working days** from a complete file; entity 5 WD. **Tracking ID** generated; customer notified of initiation; status on app/web. Incomplete sessions resumable **≤ 30 days**. Written decline reason. Geo/IP of the digital gadget. This pack: Tracking ID **on screen at Step 0**, SMS **after OTP** (placement **D**; existence **A**). |
| A-E19 | AML regs | CDD, PEP, TFS, STR, ongoing monitoring | A | Full SBP AML/CFT/CPF overlay on the wallet relationship: identify and verify; purpose; beneficial ownership where needed; **PEP** (including family/close associates) with senior approval and SoW/SoF; **TFS** ongoing not only at open; **STR/CTR** to FMU; ongoing monitoring and periodic refresh by risk. High risk → **EDD** (CCOF: recorded video KYC for non-face-to-face EDD). Anonymous or fictitious wallets are prohibited. |
| A-E20 | EMI §13 | Cross-border / inward remittance via AD + EPD approval | A | Cross-border and inward remittance rails go through **Authorized Dealers** and need **EPD / SBP** permissions as written in §13 — the EMI is not a parallel FX house. Inward remittance **limit exclusion** (up to PKR 1,500,000 for BV holders, not minors) is a separate **§14.VI** application, not an automatic product switch. Do not advertise “receive any remittance, no cap” on a Verisys 50k wallet. |

**How to use this table:** Product and UX implement the **Requirement** column without watering it down. Engineering uses **What it means** for gates, states, and “do not.” Compliance maps evidence to **Source**. Open Legal items (NADRA face BV, BPRD 04 on EMIs, mint Tracking ID after OTP) are **not** in this A-list as signed design — they live in the [guided docs open register](../docs/index.html#open).
